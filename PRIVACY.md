# Easy Quran — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Easy Quran ("the app") is a free Quran reader. It has **no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking**. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

## What stays on your phone
- **Reading data** — your last-read place, bookmarks, notes, reading log, goals, tasbeeh counts and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Location** — only if you choose "Use my current location" for prayer times and Qibla. Android asks for your permission first. The coordinates are used on the phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction and are saved only on the device. To show your area's name, the app asks the phone's built-in geocoder (Android's Geocoder service) for the place name of those coordinates. You can instead pick a city from the list, which uses no location at all.

## Internet use
The app works offline. It connects to the internet only to download content you choose, once:
- translations and dhikr audio, from `raw.githubusercontent.com` (GitHub). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address for that request; the app sends nothing else.

## Notifications
Daily reading reminders are scheduled on the phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Permissions
- Location (approximate / precise) — optional, for prayer times and Qibla, as described above.
- Notifications and "run at startup" — to show and restore your reading reminders.
- Internet — to download translations and dhikr audio.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
